## format-024: Stale-Data Mislabel Cross-Check Protocol — ACTIVATED (report 1/3)

**Motivation:** today's session caught two concrete cases (Note 2: LMT's "$587.95," actually Aug 8 data; BAH's "$129.13"/"$101-190 range," actually ~Oct 2025 data) of a WebSearch aggregator presenting stale, differently-dated data as a ticker's "current" price. Both were caught only because the researching agent happened to run a follow-up query cross-checking the figure against other dated anchors in the same pass — an ad hoc save, not a repeatable step. With AC and CV both saturated at 100/100, this project's recent experiment history shows only AS can move CS, and a mis-scored signal (scored against a wrong, stale anchor) is a direct, concrete way AS can be corrupted — arguably a more consequential risk than the narrow-event override experiments (format-012/017/018/020/021) that have dominated the recent queue.

**Rule:** before accepting any WebSearch-sourced "today" price as a candidate, cross-check its implied day-over-day or multi-day move against the most recent confirmed-dated anchor available in the same research pass. If the implied move exceeds ~15% with no corroborating same-day news to explain it, treat the candidate as a probable stale/mislabeled figure, exclude it from the confirmed-price candidate set, and fall back to PENDING/UNCONFIRMED rather than use it (or score against it). This formalizes exactly the check that caught LMT and BAH today into a standing step for every research pass, rather than relying on an incidental follow-up query. Classified as a data-source/presentation experiment (not signal-logic) — auto-evolve, no approval needed.

**cs_before (baseline):** 75.81 (today's CS). 3-report KEEP/DISCARD evaluation, reverted on DISCARD per the standard auto-evolve rule. Report 1/3 today: the rule was applied retroactively to this session's own research passes and is credited with the two catches in Note 2 (both already excluded under informal practice before the rule was formally named; today is the first report to log it as a named, standing check).
