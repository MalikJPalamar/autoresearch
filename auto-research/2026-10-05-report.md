## format-025: Anchor-Chain Price Confirmation Protocol — ACTIVATED (report 1/3)

**Category:** data-source/data-presentation refinement to the standing format-016/format-022 framework — auto-evolve, pre-approved 2026-08-20. **cs_before (baseline):** 75.86 (today's CS). **3-report KEEP/DISCARD**, reverted on DISCARD per the auto-evolve rule.

**Motivation:** today's AMAT research produced two sources that cluster tightly on price *level* ($514.39 vs $515.12, 0.14% apart) but report mutually inconsistent daily %-changes (+3.91% vs +1.47%) — strong evidence the two figures are not actually from the same session despite their coincidental proximity. Classifying this as MEDIUM dispute on level-clustering alone (as Friday's TSM/ASML/AMAT breakthrough arguably did more loosely) would have been a mistake: a real, same-session rally should produce *both* level agreement *and* %-change agreement, as Friday's TSM example demonstrated (Oct 1 close $459.20 × 1.0264 = $471.32, matching a same-day Benzinga headline — an explicit arithmetic chain back to a confirmed dated anchor).

**Rule:** a "today" price candidate may be classified at the dispute-severity tier implied by cross-source agreement (format-016) **only if** it can be arithmetically chained to a specific, previously-confirmed dated close via a stated %-change that is internally consistent across the corroborating sources. Two sources whose price *levels* cluster tightly but whose stated %-changes are mutually inconsistent (implying different base sessions) must be classified no better than HIGH, regardless of the apparent level agreement.

**Report 1/3 (today):** the rule's logic was applied prospectively to today's own AMAT classification (see Signal Changes above) — correctly keeping AMAT at HIGH dispute / WATCH rather than erroneously reconfirming it at ACCUMULATE on the strength of a coincidental, non-corroborating price cluster. 0 of the other 17 tickers presented a comparable "tight level cluster, inconsistent %-change" pattern today (most had either no close candidates at all, or candidates that disagreed on level by wide margins).
