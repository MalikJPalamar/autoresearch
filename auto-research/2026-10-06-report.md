## format-025: Anchor-Chain Price Confirmation Protocol — Report 2/3

**Rule (recap):** a "today" price candidate may only be classified at the dispute-severity tier implied by cross-source price-LEVEL agreement if it also chains arithmetically to a confirmed prior close via a stated, internally-consistent %-change across sources. Tight level-clustering with mutually inconsistent %-changes = classify HIGH regardless of apparent level agreement.
