## format-026: Duplicate-Session Stale-Feed Detection Protocol (NEW — ACTIVE, report 1/3)

**Motivation:** Today's session reproduced, for a 2nd consecutive day, the exact-duplicate stale-feed pattern first identified 2026-10-06 (NVDA/AMD/MSFT/META repeating their Sep 29 figures verbatim) — and AMAT extended its own identical-pair recurrence to a **3rd consecutive session** (Oct 5, 6, 7). This is a stronger, more specific signal than the general format-024 stale-data check (which looks for *any* old figure resurfacing): a verbatim digit-for-digit repeat of price **and** $-change **and** %-change across sessions is powerful evidence of a frozen/cached search-index artifact for that specific ticker, not just an old-but-plausible number.

**Rule:** when a "today" price candidate exactly matches (price, $-change, and %-change all identical) a figure already logged and excluded in a prior session's report for the same ticker, classify it as a **confirmed frozen-feed artifact** (stronger-confidence subset of format-024) and: (a) exclude it from the confirmed-price candidate set as usual; (b) explicitly track and report the consecutive-session count for that ticker in the report's Operational Notes; (c) once a ticker reaches **3 consecutive sessions** of this exact pattern, flag it in the report as a ticker whose WebSearch-sourced price should be treated as structurally unreliable until a genuinely new, non-duplicate figure appears — a stronger warning than the standard HIGH dispute-severity tier, since HIGH dispute from conflicting sources is a different failure mode than HIGH dispute from zero fresh data.

**Classification:** data-source/data-presentation refinement (auto-evolve, pre-approved 2026-08-20) — no approval required.
